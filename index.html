General

Identity, access and how the device advertises for configuration.

Held in RAM and back ON at every power-up. Turn off to ship a pure non-connectable beacon; power-cycle the device to reach it again.

Drops config advertising to −20 dBm every 2000 ms, so the device is only reachable within roughly 0.3–1 m. Saves battery and keeps config physically private. Beacon slots are untouched. Held in RAM, back OFF at every power-up.

The BLE address is the chip's factory address and cannot be changed.

Advertising slots

Six independent slots. Each one carries its own frame, radio power and interval.

Calibrating measured power

Open nRF Connect, hold the phone against the beacon (about 0 m) and read its RSSI. Type that number into Measured power @ 0 m above, then write the slot. The firmware converts it per frame: iBeacon subtracts 41 dB for its 1 m reference, Eddystone uses the 0 m value as-is.

Encoded as scheme plus suffix per the Eddystone spec, up to 17 bytes after the scheme.

TLM is generated by the device: supply voltage, die temperature, advertising count and uptime. Nothing to configure.

Sent as AD type 0xFF, manufacturer specific data. 0x5054 is the Tapama default.

You build the whole AD yourself. A wrong length byte means the slot will not advertise at all. Spaces are ignored.

Remaining—

Level maps 3.0 V to 100% and 2.4 V to 0%, the usable window of a 3 V coin cell under BLE load. Remaining time is level times capacity divided by estimated current — an order-of-magnitude figure, not a measurement. Use a power profiler for real numbers.

Installing an update

Download the .bin, then click Enter update mode. Open nRF Connect Device Manager, connect to this device, open Firmware Upgrade, pick the file and choose Test and Confirm. No password is involved: the device verifies the image signature and refuses anything not signed with the official key.

Update mode is active. The device accepts a firmware upload from nRF Connect Device Manager and the session lock is off. Configuration is closed here until the update finishes. Power-cycle the device to cancel.
